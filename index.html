Nonprofit Funding Calendar

A month-by-month planning tool for Rhode Island nonprofits. Use it to forecast application workload and plan ahead for cycles that come once a year.

24 confirmed deadlines · August: 6 · September: 10 · October: 4 · November: 3 · December: 1
Please read before planning around these dates. This calendar is a planning aid, not a guarantee. The Confirmed section lists opportunities whose exact deadline was published by the funder. Date To Be Confirmed lists opportunities known to open in a given month but without a published day yet. Forecast dates are our estimates based on past cycles. We assume grant cycles stay consistent — but this is a volatile moment in philanthropy. Funders may shift deadlines, pause a cycle, or end a program with little notice. Always verify directly with the funder before you invest time in an application.
